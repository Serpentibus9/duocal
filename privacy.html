DuoCal — Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

DuoCal is a small private calendar app made for two people to share one calendar. It is not a business and does not sell anything.

What DuoCal accesses

When you sign in with Google, DuoCal asks for permission to see and edit your Google Calendar and to know your email address. It uses this only to show and change the calendars DuoCal creates ("Us · DuoCal", "DuoCal · data", "⏰ DuoCal alarms") and any other calendar you choose to display.

Where your data is stored

  • All your data (events, alarms, sleep log, counters, settings) is stored in your own Google Calendar.
  • DuoCal has no server and no database. Nothing is sent to the makers of DuoCal or to anyone else.
  • Your browser keeps a temporary copy on your own device so the app opens quickly; signing out deletes it.
  • The Google access pass lasts at most one hour and is kept only for the current app session.

Who can see your data

Only you and the person you share your DuoCal calendars with in Google. Google Calendar's own sharing decides this. DuoCal does not share data with third parties, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or tracking.

Use of Google user data

DuoCal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide DuoCal's calendar features to you, is never transferred or sold, and is never used for advertising.

Removing access and deleting data

  • In DuoCal: Settings → Sign out → "Sign out + cancel access".
  • Or at Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → DuoCal → Remove access.
  • To delete your data, delete the DuoCal calendars in Google Calendar (Settings → the calendar → Delete).
